## Brand & Style

This design system channels an uncompromising, high-velocity neo-brutalist street-food energy engineered directly for craving-driven Indonesian culinary e-commerce and local eatery discovery. It fuses the visceral, aromatic heat of crushed fiery bird's eye chilies and crispy spiced fried chicken (*ayam penyet*) with punchy editorial typography, hard ink-black structural borders, and instant transaction-oriented triggers. 

The aesthetic eschews generic softness in favor of deliberate, razor-sharp geometric framing, high-contrast posterized card layouts, and tactile, high-impact CTA architecture. It speaks directly to hungry urbanites, college students, and food enthusiasts seeking rapid satisfaction via WhatsApp ordering and instant map navigation.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the blazing sizzle of `#E65100` (deep crispy orange) balanced by the fresh, pungent bite of `#1B5E20` (crushed sambel ijo deep green). 

- **Primary (`#E65100`):** Drives top-tier urgency, promotional banners, spicy-level meters, and core brand emphasis.
- **Secondary (`#1B5E20` & `sambel-ijo` `#2E7D32`):** Delivers the fresh, authentic herbal punch of freshly ground green chilies, used across quality badges, fresh herb highlights, and secondary interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#FFB300`):** Highlights crunchy crust flakes, add-ons, and pricing callouts.
- **Neutral Base (`ivory-bg` `#FDFBF7` & `ink-black` `#111827`):** Eliminates clinical sterile white in favor of a warm rustic limestone canvas, contrasted against brutalist solid ink-black typography and strict border strokes.
- **Functional Utility (`whatsapp` `#25D366`):** Reserved exclusively for one-tap WhatsApp ordering actions.

## Typography

The typography couples the raw, eccentric energy of **Bricolage Grotesque** with the industrial, mono-adjacent cadence of **Space Grotesk**. Headlines demand immediate attention with tight tracking, aggressive leading, and heavy weights reminiscent of street banners and traditional warung chalkboards. Body copy and tactical labels use Space Grotesk to preserve razor-sharp technical clarity across pricing, spice gauges, ingredient breakdowns, and address coordinates.

## Layout & Spacing

A modular 12-column fluid grid governs desktop displays, condensing down into a single-column or 2-column split feed on mobile viewports. Spacing is rhythmic and strict: sections sit within compact frames separated by structural dividers rather than expansive, empty voids.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width 1280px container, 2rem margins, 1.25rem gutters. Menu catalogs display in a punchy 3-column or 4-column card grid.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, 1.5rem outer canvas margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Sticky bottom order deck, edge-to-edge listing layouts, 1rem screen margins with ultra-dense information architecture designed for single-thumb navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

True to the neo-brutalist street aesthetic, soft ambient dropshadows and blurred glassmorphism are completely disallowed. Depth is established exclusively through:

1. **Hard Offset Ink Shadows:** Solid drop-shadows with zero blur (e.g., `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #111827`).
2. **Heavy Contrast Outlines:** Structural containers, cards, and interactive elements feature crisp `2px` or `3px` solid `#111827` borders.
3. **Layered Paper Framing:** Overlapping high-contrast panels (`#FDFBF7` over `#F7F5EE` or `#FFB300`) with distinct hairline internal dividers that mimic street food takeout vouchers and printed receipt slips.
4. **Active Depress States:** On tap or click, elements translate +2px X and +2px Y while their hard offset shadow collapses to zero, delivering immediate mechanical feedback.

## Shapes

Every component utilizes an absolute `0px` radius (sharp edge profile). Buttons, chips, image containers, dialog boxes, and floating navigation bars adhere strictly to crisp 90-degree orthogonal geometry. This zero-roundedness rule guarantees an uncompromising, street-poster grit that honors traditional warung authenticity while feeling modern and avant-garde.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Order Button (WhatsApp):** Bright `#25D366` background, solid 2px `#111827` border, 4px 4px hard black shadow, uppercase `label-lg` Space Grotesk text. Hover shifts background to `#1B5E20` with white text.
- **Action Button (Maps/Pickup):** `#E65100` background, 2px `#111827` border, crisp white text. Active state removes shadow and shifts button coordinate down-right.
- **Ghost/Tertiary Action:** `#FDFBF7` background with 2px `#111827` border and uppercase black text.

### Menu Cards
- Rigid `#FDFBF7` or `#F7F5EE` backdrop, bounded by a 2px `#111827` border and a 4px 4px solid drop shadow.
- Photography cropped to square or 4:3 ratio with sharp borders and zero corner smoothing.
- Includes embedded spice-level indicators (chili meter) and instant "Pesan Cepat" WhatsApp quick-add triggers.

### Chips & Spice Gauges
- Sharp rectangular tag geometry with 1.5px solid black borders.
- Background variations: Fire Orange (`#E65100`) for "Pedas Mampus", Sambel Ijo (`#2E7D32`) for "Sambel Original", Crisp Yellow (`#FFB300`) for "Favorit".

### Inputs & Quantity Pickers
- Text fields use `#FFFFFF` or `#F7F5EE`, heavy 2px `#111827` stroke, and mono-like Space Grotesk typing. Focus states switch border to `#E65100` with a 2px hard accent shadow.
- Quantity counters feature side-by-side squared decrement/increment toggles with zero gaps and bold ink-black boundary rules.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp square selectors with 2px `#111827` frames. Active selection displays a solid filled black or `#E65100` inner block without circular radiuses.
